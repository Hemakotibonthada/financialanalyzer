## 🎓 Understanding the Math

### Prepayment Savings Formula
```
Interest Saved = Extra Payment × Interest Rate × Remaining Time

Example:
₹10,000 extra on 18% interest, 12 months left
= ₹10,000 × 0.18 × 1
= ₹1,800 saved!

Reality check: Our calculator shows realistic savings
(usually 10-15% of prepayment amount)
```

### Why Priority Matters
```
EMI A: ₹50,000 @ 24% interest
EMI B: ₹50,000 @ 12% interest

Prepay ₹10,000 on EMI A:
→ Save ₹2,400/year in interest

Prepay ₹10,000 on EMI B:
→ Save ₹1,200/year in interest

Difference: ₹1,200 extra saved!
```

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Mistake 1: No Emergency Fund
```
"I'll prepay everything, no emergency fund"

Problem: Next emergency = New EMI
Solution: Build 50% fund first (use allocator)
```

### ❌ Mistake 2: Ignoring Small EMIs
```
"I'll only focus on the big ₹50k EMI"

Problem: Small EMI compounds, motivation drops
Solution: Close 1-2 small EMIs first (quick wins)
```

### ❌ Mistake 3: Prepaying Without Calculation
```
"I'll just pay ₹5,000 on random EMI"

Problem: Might save less interest
Solution: Use calculator, choose priority EMI
```

### ❌ Mistake 4: All Work, No Play
```
"Every penny goes to EMI, zero fun"

Problem: Burnout, can't sustain
Solution: Keep 20% for lifestyle (allocator does this)
```
