# 🚀 Bill Reminders - Quick Start Guide

## Start Using Bill Reminders in 5 Minutes!

---

## Step 1: Navigate to Bill Reminders

1. Click on **"Bill Reminders"** in the sidebar (🔔 icon)
2. You'll see the main dashboard with statistics

---

## Step 2: Add Your First Bill

1. Click the **"Add Bill"** button (top right, purple gradient)
2. Fill in the details:

   **Basic Information:**
   - **Title**: e.g., "Monthly Electricity Bill"
   - **Category**: Select from dropdown (Electricity, Water, Internet, etc.)
   - **Amount**: e.g., 2500
   - **Due Date**: Select the due date
   
   **Recurring Options:**
   - **Frequency**: Monthly (for recurring bills)
   - **Remind Me**: 3 days (before due date)
   
   **Auto-Payment (Optional):**
   - ✅ Enable Auto-Payment
   - ✅ Require Approval Before Payment
   
3. Click **"Create Bill"**
4. ✅ Bill created successfully!

## Step 4: Understand the Dashboard

### Statistics Cards (Top Row)

1. **Due Soon** (Blue)
   - Shows bills due in next 7 days
   - Total amount displayed
   - Click to filter

2. **Awaiting Approval** (Amber - Pulsing!)
   - Bills that need your approval
   - ⚠️ Action required
   - Click to filter

3. **Overdue** (Red)
   - Bills past due date
   - Total overdue amount
   - Click to filter

4. **Paid This Month** (Green)

   - Total paid amount
   - Click to filter

## Step 5: Approve Payments

### When You See "Awaiting Approval":

1. **Notification Appears**
   - You'll get a notification (bell icon)
   - "Approval Required" message
   - Click to view

2. **Open Approval Modal**
   - Click **"Approve"** button on bill card
   - Review bill details
   - Verify amount

3. **Make Decision**
   - **Approve**: Click green "Approve Payment" button
   - **Reject**: Click red "Reject" button
   - Add note (optional)

4. **Payment Processed**
   - On due date, payment auto-executed
   - Notification sent
   - Transaction created automatically
   - Next recurring bill created

## ❓ Common Questions

### Q: What happens if I don't approve?
**A:** The payment won't be processed. Bill status changes to "Rejected". You can manually pay or request approval again.

### Q: Can I change the amount before approval?
**A:** Yes! Click "Edit" to update the bill details before approving.

### Q: What if auto-payment fails?
**A:** You'll get a notification immediately. You can then pay manually using "Mark as Paid".

### Q: How do I stop a recurring bill?
**A:** Edit the bill and change frequency to "One-time", or delete the bill.

### Q: Can I see payment history?
**A:** Yes! Click "View Details" on any bill to see complete payment history.

## 🚨 Important Notes

### ⚠️ Approval Workflow
1. Bill created → Status: Pending
2. 2 days before due → Status: Awaiting Approval
3. You approve → Status: Approved
4. Due date arrives → Payment processed → Status: Paid
5. Next recurring bill created automatically

### ⚠️ Auto-Payment Requirements
- Must enable "Auto-Payment"
- Must enable "Require Approval"
- Must approve before due date
- Payment account must be configured (future feature)

### ⚠️ Recurring Bills
- Next bill created AFTER payment
- Same details as original
- New due date calculated
- Status starts as "Pending"
