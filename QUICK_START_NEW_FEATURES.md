# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - New Features

## Immediate Access Instructions

### Step 1: Start Your Application
```bash
# Backend
cd backend
npm start

# Frontend (new terminal)
cd frontend
npm start
```

### Step 2: Login
- Go to `http://localhost:3000/login`
- Login with your credentials

### Step 3: See the New Features! 

You'll immediately see:

1. **📱 Sidebar Menu (Left)** - 7 new menu items with icons
2. **🎨 Dashboard** - A colorful "New Features Available" card with 8 clickable feature tiles

## 🎯 Feature Walkthrough

### 🧠 AI & ML Insights (/ml-dashboard)
**What it does:**
- Predicts future spending patterns
- Detects unusual transactions (fraud detection)
- Recommends budget optimizations
- Shows ML model accuracy metrics

**How to use:**
1. Click "AI & ML Insights" in sidebar (purple brain icon)
2. View your spending predictions in charts
3. Check anomaly detection alerts
4. See personalized recommendations

---

### 🧮 Tax Planner (/tax-planner)
**What it does:**
- Calculates tax liability
- Compares old vs new tax regime
- Suggests deductions (80C, 80D, etc.)
- Shows tax-saving opportunities

**How to use:**
1. Click "Tax Planner" in sidebar (red calculator icon)
2. Enter your annual income
3. Add deductions (PF, insurance, home loan, etc.)
4. Compare tax regimes
5. Download tax report

---

### 🛡️ Insurance Portfolio (/insurance)
**What it does:**
- Tracks all your insurance policies
- Monitors premium due dates
- Manages claims
- Analyzes coverage gaps

**How to use:**
1. Click "Insurance" in sidebar (blue shield icon)
2. Click "Add Policy" button
3. Enter policy details (type, coverage, premium)
4. View all policies in dashboard
5. Get alerts for expiring policies

---

### 📈 Retirement Planning (/retirement)
**What it does:**
- Calculates retirement corpus needed
- Projects your savings growth
- Shows monthly income post-retirement
- Tracks retirement goals

**How to use:**
1. Click "Retirement" in sidebar (green trending up icon)
2. Click "Create New Plan"
3. Enter current age, retirement age, target corpus
4. Set monthly contributions
5. View year-by-year projections

---

### 🏢 Real Estate Portfolio (/real-estate)
**What it does:**
- Tracks property investments
- Monitors rental income
- Calculates property ROI
- Manages mortgage payments

**How to use:**
1. Click "Real Estate" in sidebar (orange building icon)
2. Click "Add Property"
3. Enter property details (address, value, purchase date)
4. Add rental income (if applicable)
5. View portfolio performance

---

### 💼 Business Management (/business)
**What it does:**
- Creates professional invoices
- Manages clients and projects
- Tracks payments
- Business analytics

**How to use:**
1. Click "Business" in sidebar (indigo briefcase icon)
2. Add clients first (click "New Client")
3. Create projects
4. Generate invoices (click "New Invoice")
5. Track payment status

---

### 🔔 Notifications (/notifications)
**What it does:**
- Centralized notification hub
- Bill payment reminders
- Budget alerts
- Multi-channel delivery (email, SMS)

**How to use:**
1. Click "Notifications" in sidebar (yellow bell icon)
2. View all notifications
3. Click "Settings" to configure preferences
4. Choose channels (email, SMS, push, WhatsApp)
5. Set digest frequency (daily/weekly/monthly)

---

### 🔍 Advanced Search (/advanced-search)
**What it does:**
- Natural language search
- Search across all financial data
- Advanced filtering
- Saved searches

**How to use:**
1. Click "Advanced Search" in Advanced menu (cyan search icon)
2. Try queries like:
   - "expenses over ₹5000 this month"
   - "income from last week"
   - "groceries less than ₹1000"
3. Use filters (date, amount, category)
4. Sort results by relevance/date/amount

---
