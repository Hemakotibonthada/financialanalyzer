## 🎯 Get Started in 3 Steps

### Step 1: Enable Authentication (1 minute)

1. Open Firebase Console:
   ```
   https://console.firebase.google.com/project/finserveassist/authentication/providers
   ```

2. Click **"Get started"** (if first time)

3. Click **"Email/Password"**

4. Toggle **"Enable"** switch ✅

5. Click **"Save"**

**Done!** Authentication is now active.

---

### Step 2: Open Your App

Visit: **https://finserveassist.web.app**

You should see:
- Beautiful landing page
- "Get Started" button
- Feature showcase
- Login/Register options

---

### Step 3: Create Your Account

1. Click **"Get Started"** or **"Sign Up"**

2. Fill in:
   - Name: Your name
   - Email: your.email@example.com
   - Password: (minimum 6 characters)

3. Click **"Sign Up"**

4. **You're in!** Dashboard loads automatically 🎉

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Issue: Can't register
**Solution:** Make sure Email/Password authentication is enabled in Firebase Console.

### Issue: Data not saving
**Check:**
1. You're logged in
2. Internet connection active
3. Firestore rules deployed (they are ✅)

### Issue: Page not loading
**Check:**
1. URL: https://finserveassist.web.app
2. Internet connection
3. Clear browser cache

### Issue: Forgot password
**Solution:**
1. Click "Forgot Password" on login page
2. Enter your email
3. Check email for reset link
4. Follow instructions
